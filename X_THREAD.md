## Post 2

The idea is simple:

1. Connect Nightly
2. Choose what you're making
3. Write a short build note
4. Bake it on-chain

OVENLOG stores a transparent Memo transaction and gives you a shareable CookieScan receipt. No account or database required.

[composer + receipt screenshot]

## Post 3

Why Cookie Chain?

A maker log only works if recording progress feels cheap and immediate.

Cookie Chain gives OVENLOG fast confirmation, tiny COOK fees, familiar Solana tooling, and a public explorer anyone can verify.

The network isn't hidden—it is the product.

## Post 4

OVENLOG also reads the chain in real time:

• current slot and block height
• live TPS and RPC response time
• connected wallet balance
• recent activity and confirmation status

Every successful entry links straight to its CookieScan transaction.
